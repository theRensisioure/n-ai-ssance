# Socratic series · Durable land

*Ground story: capture inbox → stamped land — use-cases/05.*

## Spine (mirror)

Durable means **copy by default**, boring provenance, no mystical archive.

## Sequenced prompts

**P1.** What have you deleted that you thought was “backed up” in chat or Downloads?

**P2.** Why is move-as-default dangerous for *you* specifically—not for a generic user?

**P3.** What belongs in durable land vs chat-diff archives vs never-git?

**P4.** How small can a MANIFEST be and still let future-you trust the stamp?

**P5.** When does “land it” become procrastination instead of protection?

## Seeds if stuck

1. List three capture types you actually produce.  
2. Write one MANIFEST line for a fake screenshot.  
3. State copy vs move as a one-line law.  
4. Separate land from “AI memory.”  
5. Park encryption for later.
