# Socratic series · Intent fence

*Ground story: thrash multi-goal open — use-cases/03.*

## Spine (mirror)

Intent is a **fence for scope**, not a mood journal.

## Sequenced prompts

**P1.** Outcome / surface / fence—which of the three do you skip under pressure, and what usually explodes because of that skip?

**P2.** Write the fence line for “example-app auth only” that would have blocked a rebrand detour. Keep it ugly and short.

**P3.** When is a missing intent packet a signal to interview, and when is it a signal you already know and are stalling?

**P4.** How do you want an agent to behave when you expand scope mid-turn—refuse, park, or re-fence?

**P5.** What personal cost (not product cost) shows up when dual-tracking wins?

## Seeds if stuck

1. Fill outcome only for today’s real head.  
2. Fill fence only—list three outs.  
3. Compare two past thrash days: fence present vs absent.  
4. One phrase that means “re-open fence.”  
5. Park “perfect goal ontology.”

## Done-when

You can point at a packet (or its absence) as the reason a thread stayed thin.
