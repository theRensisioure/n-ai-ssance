# Socratic series · Shipping the skill pack

## Spine (mirror)

The pack is **sterile community infrastructure**, not a private diary with a remote.

## Sequenced prompts

**P1.** What must never appear in a commit even though the repo is private?

**P2.** What does “tired of hiding” mean as a *shipping* act that still keeps life out of git?

**P3.** Who is the first stranger you imagine running `/session-map`, and what do they need in the README that you do not?

**P4.** How will you know D2 is done without reopening D1 identity thrash?

**P5.** What is the smallest public-facing sentence that is true today?

## Seeds if stuck

1. Run sterile-check before you feel ready.  
2. Write the stranger sentence in one line.  
3. List three ban-list items from memory.  
4. Pick next skill only.  
5. Park marketing.

## Done-when

Push anxiety is about quality of procedure, not fear of being seen with a mess.
